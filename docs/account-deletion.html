Delete your NutriTrack account

NutriTrack is a personal nutrition companion app. You can delete your account and all associated personal data at any time, directly in the app.

Delete in the app (immediate)

  1. Open NutriTrack and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Account.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm with your password.

Your account is deleted immediately. This cannot be undone.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email address and password credentials)
  • Your profile and username (the username becomes available again)
  • Your questionnaire answers
  • Your NutriCoach conversation history

Data stored only on your device (such as the fruit nutrition cache, which contains no personal information) is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

Request deletion by email

If you can no longer access the app, email shswww000@gmail.com from the email address of the account you want deleted, with the subject “NutriTrack account deletion request”. We may ask you to verify ownership of the account. Email requests are processed within 30 days, and you will receive a confirmation when deletion is complete.

Data retention

After deletion, no personal data is retained. NutriTrack does not keep backups of personal data beyond what is needed to complete your deletion request.
